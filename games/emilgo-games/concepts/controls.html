Look settings (concept)

Graph: turn per pixel of thumb travel (up) against thumb speed (right). Slow swipes stay on the flat start: fine aim. Fast flicks climb the curve: a 180° turn in one swipe. The dot is your last swipe.

Dead zone: the first few pixels after a touch lands are ignored, so tapping FIRE or a button never nudges the aim. It applies once per touch, not on every move (that would feel sticky).
